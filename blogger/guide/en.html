1. Getting started

You don't need to read the entire guide from the beginning. Learn the flow below and you can start using the app right away.

Get started with ShiftMate in 5 minutes
  1. Tap Entry at the top of the Shifts tab at the bottom to enter your first shift.
  2. Go to Show more → Alarm settings, turn on the work alarm for the shift you use, and check the time.
  3. Add the ShiftMate home widget to your phone's home screen.
  4. If needed, go to Show more → Share shifts → Send link to send an invitation link to a coworker.
Recommended
At first, setting up only shift entry → work alarm → home widget is enough.

2. Shifts

Enter and review your rotating shifts, such as D, E, N, and Off, in the Shifts tab at the bottom.

Enter a shift

Edit your shift schedule in Shifts → Entry.

  1. Open the Shifts tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap Entry at the top of the screen.
  3. Select the date to enter on the calendar.
  4. Tap the desired shift button below, such as D, E, N, or Off.
  5. When finished, tap Save in the upper-right corner.
Quickly enter a month's shifts in sequence

The ShiftMate shift entry screen does not select multiple dates at once. Instead, when you enter a shift for one date, the selection automatically moves to the next date. You can quickly enter a month's shifts in order, such as D → E → N → Off, while viewing your schedule.

  1. Open Shifts → Entry.
  2. Select the date to start entering.
  3. Tap the shift button for that date.
  4. When the selection moves to the next date, continue tapping the next shift.
  5. After entering everything, tap Save in the upper-right corner.
Tip
If you need to edit a date in the middle, select that date again on the calendar and continue entering directly.
Edit or delete a shift

In Shifts → Entry, select the date to edit, tap another shift, or select Delete, then tap Save.

Set up the shifts you use

Go to Show more → Shift settings to manage shift names, colors, shift hours, and the shifts you use.

You can also access this from the full settings: Show more → Full settings in the upper-right corner → Shifts and service → Shift settings.
Check shift analysis

Go to Show more → Shift analysis to review entered shifts by period. Use it to examine counts and patterns by shift type.

3. Schedule

ShiftMate displays personal events from your device calendar alongside your shifts and connects to schedule add and edit features.

Add a personal schedule

Select the desired date in the Shifts tab at the bottom, then use the + button to add a schedule. Depending on your device and operating system, the system calendar screen may open.

To view personal schedules with your shift schedule, calendar access permission is required on your device.
Set recurring schedules and schedule reminders

In the schedule editing screen, you can set repeat options, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, and schedule reminders.

Change how shifts and schedules are displayed

Go to Show more → Full settings in the upper-right corner → Display settings to adjust how shifts and schedules are displayed and their size.

4. Coworkers · Shift sharing

Connect with coworkers using an invitation link and view shared shifts together in the Coworkers tab at the bottom.

Send a shift-sharing link to a coworker
  1. Open Show more at the bottom.
  2. Select Shift sharing.
  3. Select Send link.
  4. Send the invitation link through the app you want, such as KakaoTalk or text messages.
  5. After the other person completes the connection, check the Coworkers tab at the bottom.
Check a coworker's shifts

Check connected coworkers' shifts in the Coworkers tab at the bottom. Saved content is shown first, followed by the latest data, and the latest shared shifts from the server are checked when you enter the coworker screen or change the month.

Real-time reception, which automatically delivers changes to the screen as soon as a coworker changes a shift, is a separate feature.
Manage coworkers

Use coworker management on the Coworkers screen at the bottom to add coworkers or organize their display order, filters, and connection status.

5. Work alarms

Set alarms for each shift according to the different start times for each shift type.

Turn on a work alarm
  1. Open Show more at the bottom.
  2. Select Alarm settings.
  3. Turn on the switch on the right of the shift for which you want to use an alarm.
  4. When a permission notice appears, allow the required alarm and notification permissions.
Change the alarm time for each shift

In Show more → Alarm settings, tap the desired shift card to change that shift's alarm time. Changes are applied to the settings without a separate Save button.

Set the alarm sound, vibration, and alarm screen

Go to Show more → Full settings in the upper-right corner → Alarm to check settings related to work alarms, sound and vibration, and the alarm screen.

6. Home widgets

Check your shifts from your phone's home screen without opening the app.

Add a ShiftMate widget to iPhone
  1. Press and hold the iPhone home screen and enter edit mode.
  2. Search for ShiftMate under Add Widget.
  3. Select the desired widget size and format.
  4. Place it on the home screen.
Add a ShiftMate widget to Android
  1. Press and hold the home screen and open the Widgets menu.
  2. Find ShiftMate.
  3. Add the desired widget to the home screen.
The name and location of the widget-add screen may vary slightly depending on the Android manufacturer and version.
Set what appears in the widget

Go to Show more → Widget settings to set the shifts and related options displayed in the ShiftMate widget. Actually adding the widget to the home screen is done from the iPhone/Android home screen.

7. Memos

Manage regular memos and checklist memos in the Memos tab at the bottom.

Write a memo
  1. Open the Memos tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap the + button to open a memo.
  3. Enter a title and content.
  4. Tap Save at the bottom.
Use checklist memos

If you want to check tasks item by item, change the memo type to Checklist memo and add checklist items.

Find and sort memos

Use the filters and sorting features in the memo list to quickly find the memos you need by conditions such as regular memo or checklist memo, completion status, and date.

8. Service · Analysis

Manage records separately from your shift schedule, such as annual leave, sick leave, training, business trips, lateness, and early leave, as service records.

Add a service record

Select a date on the shift calendar to open the date details screen, then tap + and select Service to add a record.

To add or edit service types, go to Show more → Full settings in the upper-right corner → Shifts and service → Service settings.
Check shift analysis and service status

Check shift patterns in Show more → Shift analysis and service records by period in Show more → Service status. In Service status, you can select a period such as this month, last month, the last 3 months, or this year.

9. Account · Data · Settings

Manage key settings and support menus in Show more at the bottom and Full settings in the upper-right corner.

Explore full settings

Manage the following areas in Show more → Full settings in the upper-right corner.

  • Shifts and service — Shift types, service, shift screen
  • Alarm — Work alarms, sound, vibration
  • Calendar — Region, first day of the week, calendars to display
  • Display settings — How shifts and schedules are displayed and their size
  • Theme — Screen mode, colors, fonts
  • Home widgets — Monthly and daily/weekly widget settings
Login and account

Login is used for automatic backup of shift data and sharing features. Before changing phones, make sure you know which account you are currently using.

Check data backup and restoration

When logged in, supported data such as shifts, memos, and service records is stored under your account. Before reinstalling or changing devices, check your network connection and login account, and log in with the same account on the new device.

Send help requests, inquiries, and feedback

In Full settings → Help and support, you can use Frequently asked questions, Contact us, Feedback and improvement suggestions, and Rate ShiftMate.
