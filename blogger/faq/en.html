1) Shift Settings · Sharing · Display

Q. The Shift I want isn’t in the list.

You can add a new Shift in Shift Settings or edit a Shift you don’t use.

Q. Only the first letter of the shift name is displayed.

If only part of a long shift name is displayed, Settings → Display Settings → Shift Size → Long shift names go to

‘Show more’ or ‘Maximum’ Select one to display more details of long shift names.

Q. If a coworker edits a Shift, is it reflected on my screen immediately?

A Shift edited by a coworker is reflected on the server, and you can check the latest Shift on the coworker screen.

Q. I want to see my family member’s or coworker’s Shift Schedule on the Shift screen.

First, have the person’s Shift shared with you.

Then Settings → Shifts, Time Off & Attendance go to, ‘Shift screen’ → ‘Shift Schedules to display’ and select the person you want.

2) Alarms · Notifications

Q. Can I use an MP3 file saved on my smartphone as an alarm sound?

Supported on Android only. Settings → Alarm → Alarm sound and MP3 button tap it, then select the file you want.

Q. The alarm sound is too quiet on my iPhone.

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Sounds & Haptics and increase Ringtone and Alerts volume Then adjust the volume and check the alarm sound again.

3) Data Storage · Backup & Restore

Q. Where is my data stored?

Shift, notes, attendance, and Share Shift information are stored on Google Cloud and automatically backed up.

Schedules use your smartphone’s calendar and are not backed up separately by ShiftMate. Depending on the settings of the connected calendar service, schedules may be synchronized to that account.

Q. How do I restore my data when switching to a new smartphone?

If you log in with the same account as before, information backed up to that account, including Shift, notes, attendance, and Share Shift information, will be restored automatically.

Schedules are not backed up separately by ShiftMate. Connect the calendar account you previously used, such as Google Calendar or iCloud, to the new device and turn on synchronization. Schedules synchronized to that account may then be displayed again.

Conversely, local calendar schedules stored only on the device are not synchronized with the account and may need to be transferred separately.

4) Schedule · Calendar · Note Management

Q. Where are schedules added to ShiftMate stored?

ShiftMate does not store schedules separately in the app; it uses your phone’s calendar.

When adding a schedule, you can choose the calendar where you want to save it from among the calendars registered on your phone. The schedule is saved to the selected calendar. For example, if Google Calendar, iCloud Calendar, and a device calendar are connected, you can select one of them to save the schedule.

Therefore, schedules shown in ShiftMate are schedules from your phone’s calendar, and the same schedules may also appear in other calendar apps using that calendar. If you edit or delete a schedule in ShiftMate, the change is also applied to the actual schedule in the selected calendar.

If you saved it to an account-synchronized calendar such as Google Calendar or iCloud, the schedule may appear again on a new device after connecting and synchronizing the same calendar account.

Q. How do I delete a schedule or note?

In the schedule or note list, swipe the item you want to delete left or right to use the delete function.

Schedules use the actual schedules in your phone’s calendar, so deleting a schedule in ShiftMate also deletes it from the calendar where it is stored.

Q. I want to hide unnecessary schedules or duplicate holidays, anniversaries, and seasonal dates.

Tap the schedule to check which calendar it is saved in . Then go to App Settings → Calendar and deselect that calendar.

The schedule is not deleted. Schedules in the deselected calendar will not be displayed in the app, and other schedules in that calendar will also be hidden.

5) Troubleshooting Shared Links

Q. ‘Open in app’ doesn’t work from a shared link.

If the link opens inside another app, the app connection may not work properly.

From the screen’s More menu, select ‘Open in browser’ or a similar option.

If that menu is not available, copy the link address and open it in an external browser such as Chrome or Safari.

6) Device Compatibility · Multiple Devices

Q. Can I share Shifts between iPhone and Android smartphones?

Yes. Regardless of the type of smartphone you use, Share Shift is available.

Shared Shifts can be viewed on the Coworker Shifts screen, and to check updated information, refresh the screen.

Q. Can I use the app on multiple devices, such as a smartphone and tablet?

We are preparing support for multiple devices so you can use the app while synchronizing data across devices. We will announce the support schedule and details later.

Data backup and restore and multi-device synchronization are separate features.

7) Coworker management

Q. Can I organize coworkers into groups?

Separate group functionality is not currently supported. Instead, you can use color coding to distinguish coworkers’ or family members’ Shifts.
