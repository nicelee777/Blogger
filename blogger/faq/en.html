1) Shift settings · Sharing · Display

Q. The shift I want is not in the list.

You can add a new shift in Shift Settings, or edit a shift type you do not use.

Q. Only the first letter of my shift name is displayed.

If a long shift name is only partially displayed, go to Settings → Display Settings → Shift Size → Long shift names.

Select “Show more” or “Maximum” to display more of the shift name.

Q. If a coworker edits a shift, will it appear on my screen right away?

A coworker’s updated shift is saved to the server, and you can check the latest shifts on the Team screen.

Q. I want to see a family member’s or coworker’s schedule on the Shifts screen.

First, ask that person to share their shifts with you.

Then go to Settings → Shifts & Time Off and choose the person under “Schedules to display” in the Shifts screen settings.

2) Alarms · Notifications

Q. Can I use an MP3 file saved on my phone as the alarm sound?

This is supported on Android only. Go to Settings → Alarm → Alarm Sound, tap the MP3 button, and select the file you want.

Q. The alarm volume is too low on iPhone.

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Sounds & Haptics and increase the Ringtone and Alerts volume. Then check the alarm sound again.

3) Data storage · Backup & restore

Q. Where is my data stored?

Shifts, notes, time-off/attendance records, and shift-sharing information are automatically backed up to Google Cloud.

Events use your phone’s calendar and are not backed up separately by ShiftMate. Depending on your connected calendar service, events may sync with that calendar account.

Q. How do I restore my data after changing phones?

Sign in with the same account you used before. Supported shift, note, time-off/attendance, and shift-sharing data will be restored automatically.

For events, check the connection and sync status of the calendar account you used before. Events stored only on the old phone may need to be moved separately.

4) Events · Calendar management

Q. I want to hide unnecessary events or duplicate holidays, anniversaries, or seasonal dates.

Open the event and check which calendar it is saved in. Then go to App Settings → Calendar and deselect that calendar.

This does not delete the event. Events from the deselected calendar will no longer appear in ShiftMate, and other events in that calendar will also be hidden.

5) Share link troubleshooting

Q. “Open in app” does not work from a shared link.

If the link opens inside another app, the app connection may not work correctly.

Use the page’s More menu and choose “Open in browser” or a similar option.

If that option is unavailable, copy the link and open it in an external browser such as Chrome or Safari.

6) Device compatibility · Multiple devices

Q. Can I share shifts between iPhone and Android phones?

Yes. You can use shift sharing regardless of which type of phone you or the other person uses.

Shared shifts are available on the Team screen. Use Refresh to check updated information.

Q. Can I use ShiftMate on multiple devices, such as a phone and tablet?

We are preparing multi-device support so that data can stay synchronized across devices. Availability and details will be announced later.

Backup/restore and multi-device synchronization are separate features.

Q. Can I organize coworkers into groups?

Separate coworker groups are not currently supported. Instead, use colors to distinguish coworkers or family members.
