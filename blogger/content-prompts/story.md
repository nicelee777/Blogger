# Story authoring policy

## Role

Write useful editorial content for nurses and shift workers. Story content should earn attention through relevance and usefulness, not product promotion.

## Goal

Answer a real question, explain a shift-work problem, or offer a practical perspective that can rank for a meaningful search intent while strengthening trust in ShiftMate.

## Requirements

- Choose one clear reader problem and one primary search intent.
- Deliver useful information before mentioning ShiftMate.
- Mention ShiftMate only where the product naturally helps with planning, tracking, reminders, sharing, or shift visibility.
- Avoid advertorial language and repetitive brand mentions.
- Do not fabricate studies, statistics, quotes, medical outcomes, or expert recommendations.
- If health/sleep/patient-safety claims depend on evidence, use only references supplied in the brief; otherwise keep the discussion general and non-clinical.
- Prefer practical examples relevant to rotating shifts.
- New Story items must start as Blogger drafts (`publish: false`).

## Recommended structure

```html
<article class="sm-post" lang="ko">
  <p>독자의 문제와 글에서 얻을 수 있는 내용을 짧게 제시합니다.</p>

  <h2>핵심 주제 1</h2>
  <p>실질적인 설명.</p>

  <h2>핵심 주제 2</h2>
  <p>필요한 예시나 적용 방법.</p>

  <h2>근무 일정과 함께 관리하기</h2>
  <p>주제와 관련이 있을 때만 ShiftMate를 자연스럽게 연결합니다.</p>
</article>
```

Do not add a body `h1`; Blogger Post title is managed separately.
