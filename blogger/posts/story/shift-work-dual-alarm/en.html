Shift work alarms—are you sure one device is enough?

Most office workers generally start work at similar times.

But shift workers are different.

You might work a Day shift today, an Evening shift tomorrow, and a Night shift the day after. Every time your shift changes, your preparation and alarm times change too.

Especially in professions like nursing, where the Shift Schedule changes every month, work alarms that match your Shift Schedule are important.

ShiftMate is designed with these shift work environments in mind and offers a dual-alarm feature that lets you use your smartphone and tablet together.


Why is alarm management difficult for shift workers?

Let’s use Day, Evening, and Night shifts as examples.

You can’t have your alarm go off at the same time on a Day shift day and a Night shift day.

Every time your shift changes,

“What time do I start work tomorrow?”

“Did I set the alarm correctly?”

you find yourself checking again.

Unexpected shift changes and personal plans can make alarm management even more complicated.

ShiftMate is a shift work calendar designed to manage schedules and notifications around your Shift Schedule.
