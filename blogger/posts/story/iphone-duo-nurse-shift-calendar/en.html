Can you use your everyday shift schedule app on the new iPhone Duo (iPhone Duo)?

A nurse’s schedule of day, evening, night, and off shifts involves more than just checking today’s start time. There is often a lot to review across the month, from the next day off and days working with coworkers to hospital training and personal appointments.

With this update, ShiftMate now works on iPhone Duo as well. Here’s how to use shifts and schedules on the unfolded screen with ShiftMate, the new name of the former “I’m a Nurse.”

1. Check today’s Shift and review your monthly schedule

Before work, quickly check whether today is a day or evening shift, and during your break, review the flow of your Shifts this month.

On iPhone Duo, unfold the screen to view your Shift Schedule and schedule in ShiftMate. When reviewing the month, look at days with consecutive Shifts and days off together to organize your personal appointments.

For example, when choosing a date to meet a friend, check your off days first and also see whether the previous day is a night shift. Checking the Shifts before and after your days off, not just the number of days off, can help you plan.

2. Customize Shift names and times for your hospital

Even with the same three-shift system, Shift times and names vary by hospital and department. Some schedules, such as two-shift work, training, and annual leave, also need to be distinguished separately.

When you first use the app, check the Shift names and start and end times to match your work environment. After entering your monthly Shift Schedule, it will be easier to check each day’s Shift.

After transferring your Shift Schedule, double-check that the order of day, evening, and night Shifts and the off dates match your original duty roster. When a Shift changes, edit that date and check the start time again.

3. Check Coworker Shifts and notes together

You often check things like, “Who am I working with tomorrow?” and “When was the training day?” while viewing your Shift Schedule.

If you use ShiftMate’s Coworker Shifts sharing feature, you can review shared coworker schedules and see which days you work together.

Organizing necessary schedules and notes together can reduce the hassle of searching for information written in different places.

Instead of sensitive information such as patient names or medical details, use notes for schedule management essentials such as personal items to prepare or training schedules.

4. Check your Work Alarm together with your Shift

For shift workers whose start times change every day, developing a habit of checking tomorrow’s Shift and alarm together can help.

Set a Work Alarm for each Shift in ShiftMate, and review the settings again when your Shift changes. Especially after an app update or device change, set a test alarm for a nearby time to confirm that it works properly.

If you also use other devices such as an iPad, keep Shift data synchronization and alarm settings separate. Check the alarm settings and permissions on each device individually.

5. Former I’m a Nurse users can use the same app

ShiftMate is the new name of the former “I’m a Nurse.” If you are an existing user who wants to manage your Shift Schedule on iPhone Duo, check the update details and supported environments in the App Store.

If you’re looking for an app that makes use of iPhone Duo’s large screen, start with the Shift Schedule you check every day. From the brief moment you check today’s start time to the time you spend organizing your monthly schedule, ShiftMate can help.
