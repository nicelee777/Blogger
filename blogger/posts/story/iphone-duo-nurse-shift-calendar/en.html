Can you also use the shift schedule app you use every day on the new iPhone Duo?

A nurse’s schedule of Day, Evening, Night, and OFF shifts is about more than checking today’s start time. There is often a lot to review across the month, including the next day off, days working with coworkers, hospital training, and personal appointments.

With this update, ShiftMate now supports iPhone Duo. Here is how to view and manage your shifts and plans on the unfolded screen.

1. Check today’s Shift and review your monthly schedule

Before work, you can quickly check whether today is a Day or Evening shift, and during your break, review the flow of your shifts this month.

On iPhone Duo, unfold the screen to view your Shift Schedule and schedule in ShiftMate. When reviewing the month, look at both consecutive workdays and OFF days to organize your personal appointments.

For example, when choosing a date to meet a friend, check your OFF days first and also see whether the previous day is a Night shift. Checking the shifts before and after a day off, not just the number of days off, can help you plan your schedule.

2. Customize shift names and times for your hospital

Even with the same three-shift system, shift times and names can vary by hospital and department. Some schedules, such as two-shift work, training, and annual leave, also need to be categorized separately.

When you first start using the app, check that the shift names and start and end times match your work environment. Once you enter your monthly Shift Schedule, it will be easier to check each day’s Shift.

After transferring your Shift Schedule, double-check that the Day, Evening, and Night order and OFF dates match your original duty schedule. When a Shift changes, update that date and check the start time again.

3. Check coworker shifts and notes together

When checking your Shift Schedule, you often ask questions like, “Who am I working with tomorrow?” and “What day was the training?”

If you use ShiftMate’s coworker shift sharing feature, you can check shared coworker schedules and see which days you work together.

Organizing necessary schedules and notes together can reduce the hassle of searching for information written in multiple places.

Instead of sensitive information such as patient names or medical details, use notes for schedule-management details such as personal items to prepare or training schedules.

4. Check your Work Alarm along with your Shift

For shift workers whose start times change every day, it helps to make a habit of checking tomorrow’s Shift and alarm together.

Set a Work Alarm for each Shift in ShiftMate, and check the settings again when your Shift changes. Especially after an app update or device change, set a test alarm for a nearby time to confirm that it works properly.

If you also use another device, such as an iPad, keep Shift data synchronization and alarm settings separate. Check the alarm settings and permissions on each device individually.

5. Get started with ShiftMate on iPhone Duo

To manage your nurse shift schedule on iPhone Duo, check the latest update details and device requirements in the App Store. Then set up your shift names and times to match your workplace.

If you are looking for an app that makes use of the iPhone Duo’s large screen, start with the Shift Schedule you check every day. From the brief moment you check today’s work to the time you spend organizing your monthly schedule, you can use ShiftMate.
