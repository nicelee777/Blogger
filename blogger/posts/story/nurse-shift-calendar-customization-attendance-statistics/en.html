To use the nurse Shift Schedule app more conveniently, explore not only work dates but also annual leave and overtime records, monthly Shift Statistics, personal schedule display, and readable calendar settings. This guide introduces five ShiftMate features with real screens and examples.

1. Mark dates with emojis

Add up to two emojis per day, then choose the marks you need by category or search.

2. Manage annual leave, lateness, and overtime records

In Attendance & Leave, you can check full-day and hourly attendance, accumulated hours, and records by type. These are personal reference records, so compare them with official HR and payroll records.

3. Analyze monthly patterns with Shift Statistics

In Shift Analysis, you can compare monthly workday trends, the number of D, E, and N shifts, and estimated hours. Keep these separate from actual settlement records.

4. Different font lists for each language

The app font list in the theme varies by language. Noto Sans KR, Hi Melody, and Dongle are examples shown on the Korean screen; the same fonts may not be available in other languages.

5. Customize the Shift Schedule screen

In Screen Settings, adjust the shift display, position, text size, and background size while previewing them.

Create a Shift Schedule that fits your schedule

Enter your shifts first, then organize emojis and attendance records by date. Check monthly statistics and adjust the font and display size to create a Shift Schedule that works for you.

Features and font lists may vary by language and platform.
