What changes when you view it on a tablet?

What matters in a shift work calendar is more than simply checking today’s Shift.

how many Night shifts you have this month,

when your next consecutive days off are,

whether your personal appointments overlap with your Shifts,

and which days require coordinating your schedule with coworkers.

With ShiftMate, on a tablet you can view

  • notes

  • upcoming Shifts

and more on a large screen.


No need to manage your smartphone and tablet separately

Even if you can use it on a tablet, it is inconvenient if your data is separate from your smartphone.

When using a smartphone and tablet together with ShiftMate, you can sync your Shift and schedule data and continue using them seamlessly.

Check today’s Shift on your smartphone on your way to work,

then organize your entire schedule for next month on your tablet at home.

Rather than a Shift Schedule limited to one device, our goal is a shift work calendar that carries across multiple screens.
