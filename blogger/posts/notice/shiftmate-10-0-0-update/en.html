We’re preparing the ShiftMate 10.0.0 update. We’re improving the interface and sync experience so ShiftMate works more comfortably across a wider range of devices, while also refining frequently used features such as shift sharing, widgets, notes, and emoji.

The following are the main improvements currently planned for version 10.0.0.

Expanded support for tablets and foldable devices

  • We’re improving layouts and usability for the larger screens of iPad and Android tablets.
  • We’re optimizing layouts for Android foldable devices such as Galaxy Z Fold so screen space can be used more effectively.
  • We also plan to support future foldable iPhone environments.

Real-time continuity across multiple devices

We’re preparing multi-device sync so shifts and key data can sync in real time across smartphones, tablets, and other devices signed in to the same account.

Manage who can view my shifts

To make it easier to answer “Who can currently see my shifts?”, we plan to add a feature that lets you see and manage the users who have access to your shared shifts.

iPhone widget improvements

We’re improving the layout and usability of iPhone widgets and plan to add a large calendar widget for iOS 27 or later.

Notes UI improvements

We’re refining the notes interface so creating, reviewing, and managing notes feels simpler and more intuitive.

Emoji improvements

  • You’ll be able to assign up to two emoji per day.
  • We’re improving emoji management with categories and search so it’s easier to find the emoji you want.

Faster app startup and screen transitions

We plan to improve app startup, loading, and major screen transition speeds for a faster and more stable overall experience.

Please note

We’re developing these features with the goal of including them in the ShiftMate 10.0.0 update. Details and availability may change during development and review, and we’ll share the release schedule once it is confirmed.

Thank you to everyone who uses ShiftMate and sends us feedback. We’ll continue improving the app to make shift and schedule management more convenient.
