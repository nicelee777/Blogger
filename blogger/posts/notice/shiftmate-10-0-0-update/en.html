Here’s an overview of the ShiftMate 10.0.0 update. With this 10.0.0 update, we improved the interface so that ShiftMate is more convenient to use not only on smartphones, but also on tablets and foldable devices. We also improved frequently used features such as multi-device shift synchronization, Share Shift management, widgets, notes, and emojis.

iPad and Android tablet support

ShiftMate has been redesigned to fit the larger screens of iPad and Android tablets.

The expanded screen space makes it more convenient to view and manage your Shift Schedule, schedules, notes, and more.

Galaxy Fold and other foldable device support

Android foldable devices such as the Galaxy Z Fold now feature an improved layout so you can use ShiftMate with both the unfolded screen and the standard screen size.

We expanded support so you can naturally view your Shift Schedule on screens of various sizes and shapes.

If a foldable iPhone is released in the future, we plan to support it as well.

Real-time shift synchronization across multiple devices

On multiple devices, including smartphones and tablets, signed in with the same account, your shift data can now be synchronized in real time.

When you enter or change a shift on one device, you can view the updated Shift Schedule on your other devices, making it more convenient to use multiple devices together.

Added My Shift sharing management

“Who is viewing my shifts right now?”

Now, you can check and manage users who have access to your shared shifts.

You can check your Shift sharing relationships more easily, making it clearer to manage the current sharing status when sharing your Shift Schedule with coworkers or acquaintances.

iPhone widget improvements

We improved the widget for checking your shifts on the iPhone Home Screen.

In particular, on iOS 27 and later, you can use a large calendar widget that lets you view more schedules at a glance.

We also improved the layout and usability of existing widgets.

Enhanced landscape and tablet support for the Home Widget

We improved the Android and iOS Home Widgets so they display naturally on various screen sizes.

  • Improved widget display in landscape mode
  • Improved widget layouts for the wide screens of tablets
  • Improved Shift schedule readability across various widget sizes

You can conveniently check your Shift schedule on tablets as well as smartphones.

Emoji improvements

We improved the emoji feature, which lets you easily mark your schedule or status on your Shift Schedule.

  • Emojis are now easier to find by category
  • A search feature
  • You can now use up to two emojis per day

You can express personal schedules and important days in more varied ways, in addition to shifts.

Improved Notes UI

We improved the interface and user flow for Notes, which lets you record information, schedules, and tasks needed during your shifts.

We refined the UI to make writing, checking, and managing notes simpler and more intuitive.

Improved app startup and main screen performance

To make ShiftMate faster to use, we improved app startup and loading and transition speeds on key screens.

We reduced unnecessary waiting when checking your Shift Schedule or navigating between screens, while also improving the overall user experience.

ShiftMate 10.0

Rather than simply adding new features, ShiftMate 10.0 focused on improving the app’s core user experience so you can use your Shift Schedule more conveniently across multiple devices and share it more reliably with coworkers.

You can continue viewing on a tablet the Shift Schedule you started on your smartphone, manage the people you share your shifts with, and quickly check the shift information you need through widgets.

We will continue improving ShiftMate so nurses and shift workers can manage their shifts and schedules more easily and conveniently.
