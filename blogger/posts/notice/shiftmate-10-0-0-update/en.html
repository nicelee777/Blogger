We are preparing the ShiftMate 10.0.0 update. We are improving the interface and synchronization features for convenient use on a wider range of devices, while also refining the usability of frequently used features such as Share Shift, widgets, notes, and emojis.

The following are the major improvements currently planned for inclusion in the 10.0.0 update.

Expanded support for tablets and foldable devices

  • iPad and Android tablets We are improving the interface and usability for their larger screens.
  • Android foldable devices such as the Galaxy Z Fold We are optimizing the layout for more efficient use of the screen.
  • In the future, foldable iPhone environments will also be supported.

Continue using ShiftMate in real time across multiple devices

Across multiple devices, such as smartphones and tablets, signed in with the same account, Shift and key data will be synchronized in real time We are preparing a multi-device synchronization feature to make this possible.

New feature to manage sharing of your shifts

To easily check “Who is viewing my shifts right now?”, we will add a feature to view and manage users who have access to your shared shifts.

iPhone widget improvements

We are improving the layout and usability of iPhone widgets, and will add a large calendar widget available on iOS 27 and later.

Notes UI improvements

We are improving the interface to make writing, viewing, and managing notes easier and more intuitive.

Emoji feature improvements

  • The emojis you can use each day up to two will be selectable.
  • Category organization and search features We are improving the feature to make it easier to find and manage the emojis you want, including category organization and search.

Improved app launch and screen transition speeds

We plan to improve the loading and transition speeds of app startup and key screens, making the overall experience faster and more stable.

Notice

These features ShiftMate 10.0.0 update are being developed with the goal of including them in the update. Details and availability may change during development and review, and we will provide another notice once the exact release schedule is confirmed.

Thank you to everyone who uses ShiftMate and sends us feedback. We will continue improving it to make managing your shifts and schedules more convenient.
