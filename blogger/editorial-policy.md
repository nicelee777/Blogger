# ShiftMate Blogger editorial policy

## Purpose

ShiftMate Blogger content should help shift workers solve real scheduling and app-use problems while building a consistent, trustworthy ShiftMate brand. Content must remain useful even when the reader is not ready to install or open the app.

## Audience

Primary readers are nurses and healthcare shift workers. Write so that other rotating-shift workers can still understand the content unless the topic is explicitly nurse-specific.

## Voice and tone

- clear, calm, practical, and respectful
- concise but not abrupt
- confident only when facts are confirmed
- helpful rather than promotional
- modern and easy to scan on mobile

Avoid exaggerated expressions such as “perfect,” “the best,” “must-have,” “revolutionary,” or unsupported superiority claims.

## Structure

- Put the reader's problem or outcome near the beginning.
- Use descriptive `h2`/`h3` headings.
- Keep paragraphs short, usually 1–3 sentences.
- Use lists only when they genuinely improve scanning.
- Use examples when they clarify a workflow.
- End with a useful next action, not a generic sales pitch.

## ShiftMate mentions

- Mention ShiftMate where it naturally helps the reader complete the task.
- In Story content, prioritize educational/editorial value and use a restrained product connection.
- In Notice and Guide content, direct product references are expected.
- Do not repeat the product name unnaturally for SEO.

## Accuracy

- Use `blogger/product-facts.md` as the default product-fact boundary.
- Use the current source file and user brief for item-specific facts.
- Never invent feature availability, release dates, user counts, testimonials, quotes, or performance outcomes.
- If details are uncertain, write around the uncertainty or flag it for confirmation instead of guessing.

## High-stakes topics

For medical, health, sleep, patient-safety, legal, financial, or regulatory topics:

- do not invent evidence or cite nonexistent research;
- do not give individualized diagnosis or treatment advice;
- use references supplied in the brief when factual claims depend on external evidence;
- otherwise keep recommendations general and practical, and clearly separate app/product guidance from professional guidance.

## Accessibility and media

- Every informative image needs concise, meaningful alt text.
- Decorative images should use an empty alt attribute when appropriate.
- YouTube embeds need a descriptive `title`.
- Links should use descriptive anchor text rather than “click here.”
- Do not autoplay media.

## Calls to action

Prefer contextual CTAs such as:

- “근무표 입력 방법을 가이드에서 확인하세요.”
- “동료 근무 공유 설정을 확인해보세요.”

Avoid pressure, urgency, or manipulative phrasing.

## Content maintenance

When a feature changes, update the Korean source first. Localized versions should then be regenerated rather than independently rewritten, unless a locale-specific editorial exception is intentionally approved.
